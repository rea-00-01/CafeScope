Insight 1: Budget and mid-range cafes dominate the classified dataset

Budget and mid-range cafes account for approximately 76.66% of cafes with classified prices.
Practical use: Supports investigating a value-conscious cafe concept.
What it cannot prove: That this segment is the most profitable or has the highest unmet demand.



Insight 2: The premium segment has the highest average rating and review volume

Premium cafes average a 3.92 rating and approximately 255 dining reviews, compared with 3.35 and approximately 40 reviews for budget cafes.
Practical use: Helps examine how pricing segment relates to reported ratings and review volume.
What it cannot prove: That higher spending causes higher satisfaction, or that premium cafes generate more revenue.



Insight 3: Malviya Nagar and Mansarovar have the largest cafe concentrations

They contain 67 and 66 cafes, respectively.
C Scheme has 46 cafes and a stronger average rating of 3.82.
Practical use: Helps map competitive concentration and shortlist neighborhoods for further analysis.
What it cannot prove: Which neighborhood is the best place to launch.



Insight 4: Lal Kothi and Durgapura have promising review-volume signals

Lal Kothi averages about 223 dining reviews per cafe; Durgapura averages about 191.
Practical use: Identifies areas worth investigating alongside C Scheme.
What it cannot prove: That review volume directly translates to footfall or sales.


Insight 5: Individual cafe benchmarks can inform competitor analysis

The Rustic Spot Cafe, Tea Connect Cafe, Curious Life Coffee Roasters, Quyu's Cafe, Chaisa, and others provide useful examples of established competitors.
Practical use: Compare their ratings, listed prices, locations, and review volumes.
What it cannot prove: Their profitability or the reasons behind their popularity.
